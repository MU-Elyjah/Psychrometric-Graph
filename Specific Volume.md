# CONCLUSION

The air pressure is directly related to the atmospheric pressure. The higher the altitude the lower the density or higher specific volume. Altitude affects both the heat transfer characteristics of air as well as the pressure volume work done on the air from the fan. It is interesting to note that higher altitudes might decrease overall power consumption of the fan do to relationship shown below:

$$ w = \int \frac{P}{d\rho}$$

However heat transfer characteristics change by the following equation:

$$ q = \rho \int c_p(T) dT $$
